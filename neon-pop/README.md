# Neon Pop

**Developer:** [Akash](https://www.linkedin.com/in/iamGnu/) · **Co-developer:** Claude Code

A calm bubble-wrap popping toy for any age. There are no timers, no score to beat and nothing to lose. It's one HTML file: no install, no downloads, no internet, and it's light enough for old laptops. Play with a controller, a keyboard, a mouse or a touch screen.

## Play

Open `../index.html` (the game picker) or this folder's `index.html` in **Chrome** or Firefox. On a controller, press any button so the browser detects it. On a phone or tablet, just tap.

## Modes

- **Sheet**: a sheet of 84 glowing bubbles. Pop them one by one, or drag across them to pop a whole row. When every bubble is popped, a fresh sheet slides in, in a new colour.
- **Drift**: bubbles of all sizes float up forever, wobbling as they rise. Pop the ones you like, and the rest just float away. Now and then a big bubble drifts by, and it bursts into little ones.
- **Sound** (Soft / Crisp / Off) changes the pop sound.
- **Colours** (Rainbow / Ocean / Candy / Mint / Sunset) changes the bubble colours. Rainbow gives each row its own colour.

A counter shows how many bubbles you have popped in total. It's saved in the browser, and it's also shown on the title screen.

## Controls

| Action | Controller | Keyboard | Touch / mouse |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move the cursor | Left or right stick | W A S D / arrows | – |
| Sheet: hop bubble to bubble | D-pad | W A S D / arrows | – |
| Pop | A / ✕ (or X / □, RB / R1, RT / R2) | Space or Enter | Tap or click |
| Pop lots | Hold A and move | Hold Space and move | Press and drag |
| Pause | Start / Options | Esc or P | II button, top right |
| Sound on/off | Back / Create | M | Sound in the menu |

## Tips

- In Drift mode, you can hold a finger (or the mouse button, or A) still and let bubbles float into it.
- Several fingers at once work too.
- A small rumble on the controller, or a tiny buzz on phones that support it, comes with each pop.

## Options

- Add `?fps` to the URL for a frame counter, or `?low` for very weak machines.
- Tuning values (sheet size, bubble sizes, drift speeds, cursor speed and colour themes) are in `CFG` and `THEMES` at the top of the script.
