# Neon Drive

**Developer:** Akash · **Co-developer:** Claude Code

A calm neon road racer, seen from behind the car. It's one HTML file: no install, no downloads, no internet, and it's light enough for old laptops. Nobody crashes: bumping a car or a lamp post only slows you down.

## Play

Open `../index.html` (the game picker) or this folder's `index.html` in **Chrome** or Firefox. You can play with a controller, a keyboard, a mouse or a touch screen. If you use a controller, press any button on it so the browser detects it.

## Modes

- **Race:** 3 laps against five rivals. You start at the back of the grid and pass them one by one. Your best race time and best lap are saved for each track.
- **Cruise:** an endless drive with no rivals and no timer, just a little slow traffic. Strings of floating lights hover over the road. Drive through them for soft chimes. The kilometres you drive add up on the title screen.

## Tracks

- **Sunset Coast:** gentle curves past palm trees under a striped sun. A good first drive.
- **Midnight City:** neon streets, towers and sweeping bends.
- **Aurora Pass:** big hills and tight bends under the northern lights.

## On the road

- Glowing arrows on the road are **boost pads**. Drive over one for a short burst of speed.
- Bends push you toward the outside. Steer into them, and ease off the gas in the tight ones if you need to. Chevron signs warn you before the tighter bends.
- Off the road you slow right down. Bumping into the back of a car slows you to its speed.

## Options

- **Rivals** (Easy / Normal / Fast) sets how fast the other cars drive. On Normal and Fast they also aim for the boost pads. The game starts on Easy.
- **Assist** (On by default) makes bends push you less and steers you back onto the road if you drift off. Turn it off for more of a challenge.
- **Sound** turns the soft engine hum and the chimes on or off.

## Controls

| Action | Controller | Keyboard | Mouse / touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gas | RT / R2 or A / ✕ | ↑ or W | Hold the mouse button (on touch, the gas is automatic) |
| Brake | LT / L2, B / ○ or X / □ | ↓, S or Space | Let go |
| Steer | Left stick or D-pad | ← → or A D | Hold to the left or right of the car |
| Pause | Start / Options | Esc or P | ‖ button (top right) |
| Sound on/off | Back / Create | M | Speaker button (top right) |

All the menus work with any of these: click or tap an option to choose it. The game pauses when you switch tabs or unplug the controller, and it keeps the screen awake while you play.

## For slower computers

Add `?low` to the address (for example `index.html?low`). The game then draws less of the road ahead at a lower resolution. Add `?fps` to show the frame rate.
